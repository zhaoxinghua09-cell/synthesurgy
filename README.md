## Definition / 定义

**EN**
Synthesurgy is the engineering of synthesis: converting scattered factors into coherent structures while preserving the integrity of every source — enumeration, categorization, structure-building, and validation. Synthesis retains full definitions and evidence provenance; it is not compression.

**ZH**
梳理综合工程术是把分散因素收拢为整体结构：清单化→归类→建结构→验证。梳理不等于压缩，归并时保留完整定义与证据源。
